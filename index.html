Context-Free Grammar (CFG)

Expr   → Term (( '+' | '-' ) Term)*
Term   → Power (( '*' | '/' ) Power)*
Power  → Factor ( '^' Power )?
Factor → '+' Factor | '-' Factor 
       | NUMBER | '(' Expr ')'
